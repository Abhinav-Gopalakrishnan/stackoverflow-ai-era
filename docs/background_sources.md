# Background Sources

This document details the background context and industry research collected to contextualize structural changes in Stack Overflow question volume and community dynamics between 2019 and 2026.

---

## 1. Credible Background Sources

### Source 1: Stack Overflow Traffic & Question Volume Shifts
* **Title:** Stack Overflow drops to 1,442 questions in July, down 99% from 2014 peak
* **Publisher / Author:** Luis Rijo
* **Date:** August 2026
* **URL:** https://ppc.land/stack-overflow-drops-to-1-442-questions-in-july-down-99-from-2014-peak/
* **Key Fact Used:** Site-wide yearly question volume declined steeply from 1,336,266 in 2022 to 108,981 in 2025, dropping to 1,442 questions in July 2026 compared to a historical peak of 207,204 questions in March 2014.


### Source 2: Decline of Stack overflow began before AI
* **Title:** Stack overflow is almost dead
* **Publisher:** The Pragmatic Engineer
* **Date:** May 2025
* **URL:** https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/stack-overflow-is-almost-dead/
* **Key Fact Used:** While the release of ChatGPT closely aligns with a sharp decline in community activity, question volume decreases began well before November 2022. 2014-2022 shows a clear decline over the ages with a minor spike due to the pandemic in 2020.

### Source 3: Impact of AI on developer forums
* **Title:** Generative AI Degrades Online Communities
* **Authors:** Gordon Burtch, Dokyun Lee, and Zhichen Chen
* **Publisher:** ACM
* **Date:** Feb 2024
* **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3624732
* **Key Fact Used:** ChatGPT had driven an approximate 12% reduction in average daily Web visits to StackOverflow.com. ChatGPT’s impact on Stack Overflow participation varies significantly across topics, aligning with its expected performance based on available training data. Open-source tools and general purpose programming languages(like Python, R) showed a larger decline in participation in comparison to closed-source frameworks and technologies(like Spring Framework, Azure, AWS).
