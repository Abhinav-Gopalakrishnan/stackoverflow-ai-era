# Adhikkesh: Review 2 viva

- Why MASE? It compares error to the one-step naive error in the training history; smaller is better.
- Why the Review 1 figure changed? The old one-week score was tuned on test weeks and called 100−MAPE accuracy; this evaluation fixes those problems and includes baselines.
- What is an origin? The first forecast week, so all training observations are earlier.
- Why intervals? Point forecasts hide uncertainty; measured coverage shows whether bands are reliable. Our bands under-cover and we report that.
- Did AI cause the change? This observational study shows timing/associations, not proof of cause.
- Why some tags have no class? Percentage change from a last observed count of zero is undefined.
